# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in QuakeImagery a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologise to people affected by mistakes, and learn.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the scientific integrity of the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align
with this Code of Conduct. Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise
unacceptable behaviour should be reported privately to the project maintainer
through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ZuhairQuakes). Reports will be
reviewed promptly, fairly, and with respect for the reporter's privacy.
